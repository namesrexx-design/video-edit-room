# General social content — reusable direction

The project’s display title is **Dream Garage**. Public posts in this template carry no episode title, LYFE wording, or owner name.

The subject is the viewer’s world: recognizable characteristics, traits, habits, issues and everyday problems. A viewer should recognize a person or themselves without needing to watch a film. Use one concrete observation, a playful exaggeration when it fits, and a natural tag/share invitation. For clutter or feeling overwhelmed, add an offer to help rather than a put-down.

The centered portrait provides continuity. Keep the photo, colors, typography and placement stable. Change the hook, examples, voice script and CTA. Use the same underlying idea across the scrolling post, carousel, photo post and standalone text post. Film clips are optional and not required.
