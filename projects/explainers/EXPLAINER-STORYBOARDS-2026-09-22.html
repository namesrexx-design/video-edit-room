The visual bible: same world in all ten

ElementRule
The agentsSmall LYFE-teal figures, same four every time, from ONE approved character sheet. Generated once, approved by Rexx, then reused as the reference in every clip. No clip is generated before the sheet is approved.
The surfaceA real desk or table. Clay, olive, sand, cream. Warm window light. Never a flat AI-green background.
The folderThe Google Drive folder drawn as an open box on the desk. Labeled sub-folders appear as the agents sort.
The pilePer module, real-looking items: photos, envelopes, lab pages, menu cards. Nothing readable. No names, numbers, addresses, logos of others.
RexxNot in frame. His voice carries it. (Real footage of him is for the walk-and-talk films, not these.)
CameraStatic, square-on or a flat overhead. No push, no zoom. Cuts land on motion: an agent lifting an item, a folder closing.
CaptionsOn-screen text is the 2 to 4 words in each card, at eye level, sharing a keyword with the voice line.
End cardOn S06 of these module explainers only, under the module name, one small line: Try it free · biz-box.io/lyfe. Text only, not spoken. ⛔ Not on the entertainment / Signs character reels: Rexx 2026-09-22, "those posts launch clean first, then we add conversion once they perform."
Generationseedance_2_0 · 9:16 native · 1080p · bitrate high · generate_audio false · static camera. Hand over what the model returns, untouched.
